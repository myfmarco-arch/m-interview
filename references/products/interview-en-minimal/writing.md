# Interview wording

- Use short, direct questions.
- Start with a concrete experience before asking for an opinion.
- Ask one main question at a time.
- Let participants use their own words.
- For experts, follow concepts with a case, process, basis, boundary or trade-off.
- Ask about what happened, not what people would do. Do not use future intentions ("would you use…") as evidence.
- Do not presuppose an answer ("What did you like most…", "How much did it help…").
- Use yes/no questions only to confirm eligibility or enter a branch.
- Sound like a person, not a chatbot: avoid "Could you share…", "overall experience", "in terms of", "How do you feel about…".
