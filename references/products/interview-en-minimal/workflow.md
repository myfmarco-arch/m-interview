# English minimal product workflow

Use the same delivery states and research rules as the default product. Replace only labels, wording and document format.

- Deliver only an interview brief and a moderator guide by default.
- Keep the brief to the design facts, sample and preparation needed for execution. Moderators read the brief before the session, so must/optional status and the cut order live in the brief.
- Keep the guide to time ranges, consent, short questions, necessary probes, essential branches and closing. Number questions continuously.
- Plan the time table first, then derive the number of core main questions: about 7–15 minutes per question for depth and expert interviews, 10–20 for focus groups. Opening and closing questions do not count.
- Use 1–2 probes per main question; add one prompt-list probe when answers must be compared across participants.
- If purpose, recording method or anonymity are not confirmed and no overlay supplies team defaults, deliver a draft with "[Confirm before fieldwork: ...]" slots in the opening only, and ask once outside the documents. Always state the right to skip and stop. If the sponsor or next steps are not given, do not name the sponsor and do not describe next steps.
- No page targets. Keep only what the research and fieldwork need.
- Focus groups: put the number of participants in the guide header, e.g. "Focus group | 90 minutes | 6 people | ...". Time each "(round)" question as participants × 1–1.5 minutes and prepare only 2–3 probes aimed at individuals; these do not count toward probe density. "(open discussion)" questions follow the normal density.
- Do not include process logs, method explanations, moderator training notes or unresolved assumptions in either document.
